We're a creative studio building apps, games, and experiences designed to make people curious, make them smile, and give them something worth coming back to.

Our Story

One company.
Plenty of ideas.

Many Hats is built around a simple belief: you don't have to make just one kind of thing. Great ideas can come from different places, speak to different people, and take different forms.

That means one day we might be building a game. Another day, an educational app. Another day, an experience we haven't thought of yet.

The common thread is the way we build: with curiosity, creativity, care, and a commitment to making something people are glad they found.
